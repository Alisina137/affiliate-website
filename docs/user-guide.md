# User Guide

## Getting Started

### Browsing Products
1. Visit the homepage
2. Use the search bar to find products
3. Browse categories from the navigation menu

### Reading Reviews
1. Click on a product
2. Scroll to see the review
3. Check pros, cons, and ratings

### Comparing Products
1. Navigate to the Comparisons page
2. Select products to compare
3. View the side-by-side comparison table

### Finding Best Of Lists
1. Go to the Best Of section
2. Browse curated product lists
3. Read expert recommendations

### Using Buying Guides
1. Navigate to the Guides section
2. Read step-by-step guides
3. Follow recommendations

## Account Management

### Creating an Account
1. Click "Sign Up"
2. Enter your email and password
3. Verify your email

### Signing In
1. Click "Sign In"
2. Enter your credentials
3. You'll be redirected to the homepage

### Resetting Password
1. Click "Forgot Password"
2. Enter your email
3. Follow the reset link

## Newsletter
1. Enter your email in the newsletter form
2. Click "Subscribe"
3. Receive updates and exclusive content
